# RunGrow Month 1, Day 1 Operating Charter

**Plan period:** 18 November 2026 to 17 November 2027  
**Day 1:** 18 November 2026  
**Accountable executive:** Founder  
**Execution coordinator:** Digital CEO (once production-connected)

## 1. Company objectives

RunGrow has two independent 12-month objectives:

1. Generate **INR 5,00,00,000 in business revenue** from product sales and subscriptions.
2. Close and receive **INR 5,00,00,000 in external investment capital**.

Investment proceeds are not revenue. Pipeline, bookings, signed but uncollected commitments, and investor indications are not recognised as completed outcomes.

These are management targets, not forecasts or guarantees.

## 2. Starting assumptions and evidence status

- The founder has stated that the required operating systems and software products across RunGrow's verticals are built.
- Commercial traction is early-stage.
- Existing customer counts, paid subscriptions, recognised revenue, cash collections, churn, pricing, gross margin, CAC, LTV, and current cash runway have not yet been independently reconciled in this repository.
- Product functionality, commercial readiness, pricing, and compliance claims must be verified against the live products and approved product documentation before external claims are made.

## 3. Product portfolio and planning allocations

The initial portfolio contains seven commercial product lines:

1. Photo Studio Growth OS / Growth Pro
2. Photo Studio Billing Software
3. Clinics / Counter Software
4. Entrepreneur / Instagram Business Tools
5. Pharma Software & Utilities
6. Artists' Business Tools
7. Growtoys

The proposed product revenue allocations are recorded in `data/workforce/product-portfolio.json`. They are planning allocations only. The portfolio owner must revise them using actual pricing, demand, margins, capacity, and conversion evidence.

## 4. Operating model

RunGrow operates as one company, not seven assumed independent companies.

- The Founder retains ownership, strategy, capital decisions, and reserved approvals.
- The Digital CEO coordinates company-wide execution, reporting, cross-functional priorities, and escalations.
- Corporate functions are shared across products.
- Each product has one accountable Product Growth Manager.
- Specialist employees may serve multiple products only with explicit assignment, workload, permissions, and outcome ownership.
- External customer tenants are separate from RunGrow's internal organisation and from one another.

## 5. Day 1 priorities

1. Preserve the existing static marketing website and its component-oriented architecture.
2. Inspect and validate the existing AI Workforce branch before extending it.
3. Establish the product registry as the authoritative portfolio record.
4. Define the company hierarchy, employee contracts, reporting lines, and decision rights.
5. Establish a financial and commercial baseline from source systems.
6. Start commercial execution immediately; workforce engineering must not delay prospecting, demos, customer follow-up, or investor preparation.

## 6. Financial reporting definitions

The Finance function must report separately:

- Recognised business revenue
- Invoiced revenue
- Cash collected
- Deferred revenue
- Refunds and credits
- Recurring revenue and one-time revenue
- Gross margin and direct delivery costs
- Operating expenses and cash runway
- Sales pipeline, weighted pipeline, bookings, and conversion
- Investment pipeline, signed commitments, completed closings, and capital received

No single metric may be substituted for another.

## 7. Governance and reserved approvals

Autonomous execution is limited to the employee's assigned permissions and approved policies.

Human approval is required for, at minimum:

- Non-standard pricing, discounts, and contractual commitments
- Investor terms, securities issuance, valuation commitments, and fundraising representations
- Payments, refunds, transfers, and bank-detail changes
- Sensitive data exports and access/role changes
- Production deployments and destructive operations
- External campaign activation or outbound communication where policy requires approval

Every approval must be attributable, tenant-scoped, time-bounded, auditable, and revalidated at execution time. The requester cannot approve their own request.

## 8. Day 1 exit criteria

Day 1 is complete when:

- The portfolio has seven registered product lines with explicit validation status.
- The proposed revenue allocation sums to INR 5 crore and is clearly marked as a planning target.
- The operating hierarchy and approval boundaries are documented.
- The current AI Workforce implementation gaps are recorded accurately.
- A baseline data collection owner and source are assigned for each financial and commercial metric.
- The next engineering changes are made on a dedicated branch and do not overwrite the existing open foundation PR.

## 9. Non-goals

- Rebuilding already-built products without evidence of a commercial or reliability need.
- Treating every market segment as a separate legal entity or independent company.
- Inventing customer numbers, product capabilities, testimonials, investor interest, or performance outcomes.
- Counting investment proceeds as product revenue.
- Enabling uncontrolled agent access to customer data or external communication channels.
