# RunGrow Sales Operating System

## Operating model

RunGrow operates a hybrid, AI-led revenue organisation. AI agents handle repeatable research, enrichment, qualification, routing, preparation, follow-up drafting, CRM hygiene and customer lifecycle signals. Human sellers own discovery, commercial judgement, demonstrations, negotiation, commitments and strategic relationships.

RunGrow remains an independent multi-product software business. Every product keeps its own codebase, tenancy boundary, product experience, pricing catalogue and customer data domain.

## Segment coverage

Use vertical pods rather than hiring one salesperson for every industry. Initial pods:

1. Creative & Studio: photography studios, makeup/bridal artists, artists and creators.
2. Health & Pharmacy: clinics, pharmacies and healthcare retail. Product claims, data handling and integrations require specialist review.
3. Commerce & Retail: online sellers, organic sellers and independent retailers.
4. Experiences & Services: food, hospitality, home/lifestyle, education and professional services.

Each pod has one accountable AE and shared SDR, solutions, marketing and customer-success capacity. Rebalance headcount using qualified pipeline and conversion data, not vanity lead volume.

## Sales stages and exit criteria

1. Captured: valid contact channel and source recorded; consent and communication preferences preserved.
2. Enriched: business type, geography, size band, current workflow and product interest sourced or explicitly marked unknown.
3. Qualified: ICP fit, problem, urgency, decision process and budget range assessed; no invented fields.
4. Discovery booked: calendar event confirmed with owner, attendees and agenda.
5. Discovery complete: pain, impact, workflow, stakeholders and success criteria recorded.
6. Demo / evaluation: product-specific demonstration or trial plan delivered; evaluation owner and date agreed.
7. Proposal: approved package, scope, price, terms and validity period recorded.
8. Negotiation: objections, requested concessions and approval trail captured.
9. Closed won / lost: reason, competitor or status, contract/payment reference and next action recorded.
10. Onboarding: customer workspace handoff, activation milestones and owner assigned.
11. Adoption / renewal: usage, support health, value outcomes, renewal date and expansion signals tracked.

A stage cannot advance without its required fields. Lost, disqualified, opted-out and duplicate records remain auditable.

## Operating cadence

- Daily: new-lead SLA, overdue follow-ups, meetings today, stalled opportunities and support escalations.
- Weekly: pod pipeline review, stage conversion, ageing, forecast changes, win/loss themes and campaign quality.
- Monthly: segment economics, CAC/payback, retention, expansion, partner contribution, pricing and capacity.
- Quarterly: ICP and territory review, product feedback, sales capacity and compensation review.

## KPI framework

Measure by product, segment, channel, owner and cohort:
- Speed-to-first-human-response and speed-to-first-meaningful-response
- Lead-to-qualified, qualified-to-meeting, meeting-to-opportunity and opportunity-to-win
- Pipeline coverage, stage ageing, forecast accuracy and sales-cycle duration
- Average contract value, recurring revenue, discount rate and gross margin
- Trial activation, time-to-value, onboarding completion and product adoption
- Renewal, gross revenue retention, net revenue retention and churn reasons
- Opt-out, complaint, bounce and data-quality rates

Targets must be baselined from actual RunGrow performance. Do not fabricate conversion benchmarks or promise a 10x outcome.

## Guardrails

- AI may research, classify, draft, recommend, schedule within authorised availability and update permitted CRM fields.
- AI must identify itself as an AI assistant when asked and must not impersonate a human employee.
- No autonomous price changes, discounts, contractual promises, legal/security claims, medical claims or binding commitments.
- No outbound messages without a lawful basis, channel-specific consent/permission and suppression-list checks.
- Respect DND, opt-out, quiet hours, rate limits and channel policies; record consent source, timestamp, scope and withdrawal.
- Human approval is required for proposals, non-standard terms, sensitive-sector claims, escalated complaints and high-value opportunities.
- Tenant-scoped access, least privilege, immutable audit events, encrypted secrets and retention/deletion controls are mandatory.
