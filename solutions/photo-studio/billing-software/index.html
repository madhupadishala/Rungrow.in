Planned product scope

Billing workflows built around photography projects.

The proposed modules separate the commercial stages of a shoot, from the first estimate through final reconciliation.

01 / QUOTATIONS

Event and package quotes

Reusable package structures, configurable line items, add-ons, deliverables and quote revisions.

02 / INVOICING

GST and non-GST invoice workflows

Invoice formats and tax fields designed around applicable business registration and tax rules, subject to professional validation.

03 / COLLECTIONS

Advance and milestone tracking

Track booking advances, intermediate milestones, balance due and receipts. Payment tracking is distinct from payment-gateway collection.

04 / PROJECT FINANCE

Shoot-level costs and profitability

Organise project expenses, freelancer payouts and estimated-versus-actual shoot economics.

Important distinctions

Clear product boundaries.

Payment tracking

Recording an advance, milestone or balance received is an accounting workflow.

Payment links

Creating a payment link requires a supported provider integration and merchant onboarding.

Gateway collection

Collecting funds, settlement reconciliation and refunds require a live payment gateway integration.
